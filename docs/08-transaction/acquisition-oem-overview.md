# Acquisition / OEM Overview

Ordo Payroll Core can be structured as a technology asset in several ways. This page
describes the **possible shapes** of a transaction. It does **not** state pricing,
valuation, or definitive legal terms — those are handled privately.

## Ordo vs Ordo Payroll Core

- **Ordo** is the SaaS/ERP-style operational product.
- **Ordo Payroll Core** is the payroll + compliance **engine** underneath it.

A buyer can acquire or license **only the engine**, integrating it behind their own
product, without taking the Ordo end-user application. See
[integration architecture](../02-architecture/integration-architecture.md).

## Possible structures

| Structure | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Technology / IP acquisition** | Transfer of the engine and its intellectual property. |
| **Exclusive license** | Exclusive right to use/integrate the engine within a defined field or territory. |
| **Non-exclusive OEM** | The engine embedded inside the buyer's product, alongside other licensees. |
| **White-label** | The engine delivered under the buyer's brand. |
| **Hosted payroll API** | The engine consumed as a service through the payroll-core contract. |
| **Dedicated deployment** | A deployment isolated to a single buyer. |

## What a buyer is evaluating

- A payroll/compliance **engine** covering the core Brazilian domains, with a
  bitemporal rule model and a no-silent-fallback posture.
- A **canonical integration model** usable without adopting the Ordo product.
- A **validation corpus** (longitudinal engine-vs-oracle, persistence certification)
  and audit machinery suitable for regulated environments.
- Honest, documented **boundaries** of the validated scope.

## Due-diligence path

1. **Public showcase** (this repository) — understand the asset, its architecture,
   its validation, and its limits.
2. **NDA** — access the private data room.
3. **Private data room** — the product & technology dossier, validation dossier,
   integration dossier, IP asset inventory, detailed incident register, and evidence
   matrix, sufficient to conduct technical, legal and commercial due diligence.
4. **Transaction structuring** — pricing, terms and scope, handled privately.

## What is not on this page

No valuation, no price, no definitive legal terms, and no customer references. Those
belong to the private process, not the public showcase.
